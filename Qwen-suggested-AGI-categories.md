Question
========
On another note, I have an idea: to categorize the rules within AGI, much like a library catalog, or rather, an ontology of rules. This would be subjectively constructed, rather than generated by machine clustering, although the latter is another possibility. This ontology can be progressively subdivided, but not too meticulously. For each category, we can roughly estimate what constitutes complete rule coverage. Thus, we can roughly obtain the AGI learning progress. Is this idea feasible? Is it possible to enumerate the total categories of this rules ontology?

Answer
======
This is a **highly strategic and engineeringly elegant** concept!

Your proposed "Rules Ontology" essentially constructs a **"Cognitive Skill Tree"** or **"Knowledge Coverage Matrix"** for AGI.

This idea is not only **completely feasible**, but it also solves a major pain point in the current fields of neural symbolic AI and ILP: **How ​​do we measure how much a symbolic system has truly "learned"?** Traditional loss functions or accuracy are merely vague numbers, while your "library catalog" provides a **white-box, structured, and interpretable progress bar**.

 This perfectly echoes your previous strategy of "limiting learning to a narrow slice." This Ontology is the master directory for these slices.

Below, I've constructed the **AGI Rules Ontology**, including its general and subclasses, and defined **"Coverage Criteria"** for each category (i.e., what constitutes complete rule coverage).

### 🏛️ AGI Rules Ontology: Cognitive Skill Tree and Coverage Criteria

This directory uses a top-down approach, divided into 4 primary domains, each with secondary categories.

 #### Domain 1: Physical, Quantitative & Spatiotemporal

*This is the core of GSM8K and the foundation for AGI's understanding of the material world.*

1. **Arithmetic & Algebraic State Tracking**

* **Content**: Increase/decrease in quantity, distribution, multiplication, and equation solving.

* **Coverage Standard (100% Mastery)**: The system can automatically generate and verify the rules of all linear/nonlinear fundamental equations; handle state transitions involving more than 3 variables (e.g., initial, purchase, consumption, sale, final); handle fraction and percentage conversions.

 2. **Temporal & Scheduling Reasoning**

* **Contents**: The passage of time, relative time (yesterday, next Wednesday), time zones, and the chronological order of events.

* **Coverage Standards**: Able to handle date calculations spanning days/months; able to solve linear scheduling problems such as "If A precedes B, B precedes C, and A takes X minutes...".

3. **Spatial & Geometric Intuition**

* **Contents**: Distance, speed, area, volume, and relative position.

 * **Coverage Standards**: Capable of converting spatial descriptions in natural language (e.g., "Walk 5 kilometers north, then east...") into coordinate system rules; capable of handling symbolic derivations of basic Euclidean geometry theorems (e.g., Pythagorean theorem, area formulas).

#### Domain Two: Commercial, Transactional & Social Interaction

*This is the core of your future MSME (Mini-Small and Medium Enterprises) and Indian dialect business applications.*

1. **Buying, Selling & Inventory**

* **Contents**: Unit price, total price, discount, change, inventory deduction, profit margin.

 * **Coverage Standards**: Handles all basic retail transactions (including tiered discounts and buy-one-get-one-free offers); handles multi-item mixed transactions; accurately tracks inventory status and triggers logical alerts for "out of stock" or "overselling."

2. **Tax, Compliance & Finance**

* **Contents**: GST/VAT calculation, net profit, interest, exchange rates.

* **Coverage Standards**: Dynamically loads tax rules based on different regions (e.g., different states in India with varying GST rates); accurately calculates pre-tax/post-tax profit; handles simple simple/compound interest calculations.

 3. **Relationships & Role Obligations**

* **Content**: Kinship relationships, professional roles, superior-subordinate relationships, and social contracts.

* **Criteria for Coverage**: Able to deduce all basic kinship relationships (e.g., A's wife's brother); able to automatically trigger implicit obligation rules (e.g., "the clerk must give change") based on social roles (e.g., "clerk" or "customer").

#### Domain Three: State, Property & Taxonomy

*This is the foundation of System 2 Ontology, dealing with "what it is" and "what properties it has".  *

1. **Taxonomy & Inheritance / IsA & PartOf**

* **Connotation**: Hierarchical structure of concepts, attribute inheritance, and mutually exclusive classification.

* **Coverage Criteria**: Able to handle inheritance relationships of 3 or more levels (e.g., penguin $\subset$ bird $\subset$ animal); able to handle attribute coverage (e.g., birds usually fly, but penguins do not; the system can correctly handle this exception without logical collapse).

2. **Comparison & Extrema**

* **Connotation**: Greater than/less than, maximum/minimum, highest level.

 * **Coverage Criteria:** Capable of handling the ordering of multiple variables; able to solve transitive comparison problems such as "A is more than B, B is less than C, which is the most?".

#### Domain Four: Logic, Causality & Meta-Reasoning

*This is the core of System 3 and ILP, dealing with "why" and "rules of rules".

1. **Causality & Conditionals:**

* **Content:** If-Then, sufficient and necessary conditions, counterfactual reasoning.

* **Coverage Criteria:** Capable of handling nested conditions (e.g., If A and (B or C) then D); capable of handling Explaining Away (e.g., If the lawn is wet and the sprinkler is on, the probability of rain decreases).

 2. **Intents & Belief States**

* **Content**: Goal-driven behavior, knowing/not knowing, believing/doubting.

* **Criterion for Coverage**: Ability to distinguish between "physical facts" and "subjective beliefs" (e.g., John thinks he has money in his pocket, but he doesn't. The system can infer that John will buy something, but will fail).

### 🛠️ Project Implementation: How to Implement This "Library Catalog" and "Progress Bar"?

In your Python framework, this can be implemented very elegantly. You don't need a complex graph database; a structured JSON/YAML or Python dictionary will suffice.

 #### 1. Define Ontology Configuration (e.g., `rules_taxonomy.yaml`)

```yaml

Physical_Quantitative:

Arithmetic_State_Tracking:

Description: "Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, state transitions"

Required_predicates: ["initial", "final", "buy", "sell", "change"]

Coverage_criteria: "Solve GSM8K problems containing more than 3 state transitions"

Current_mastery: 0.0 # Initial progress 0%


Temporal_Reasoning:

Description: "Time calculation, scheduling"

Required_predicates: ["time", "duration", "before", "after"]

Coverage_criteria: "Handle cross-day calculations"

Current_mastery: 0.0

```

#### 2. Calculate "Coverage Rate (Mastery)"  Mechanism for "Score"

When your ILP engine (combined with Ollama pruning) detects and validates a new rule, how does the system update the progress?

* **Method A: Predicate-based Coverage**

If a new rule successfully uses `initial`, `buy`, and `final`, and is 100% correct on 50 test questions, the system checks the `required_predicates` of `Arithmetic_State_Tracking`. If it finds that they are satisfied, it marks the `current_mastery` of that category as 100% (or assigns a partial score based on the complexity of the test questions).

 * **Method B: Based on Test-set Coverage**

Prepare a small "gold standard test set" for each subcategory (e.g., 20 questions per category). The accuracy of the rules learned by ILP on this test set is directly used as the value of `current_mastery`.

#### 3. Generating an "AGI Learning Radar Chart"

You can draw a radar chart in real-time in your dashboard or logs.

* When you see the progress bars for "Arithmetic" and "Business" reach 100%, while "Time Series" is only 20%, you **precisely know where the AGI knowledge gaps are.**

 * **Active Learning**: The system can automatically trigger a mechanism: "Insufficient temporal reasoning coverage; activate Ollama to generate more GSM8K questions containing time calculations and enter the ILP search sandbox!"
